slug: california-mussel
commonName: California Mussel
scientificName: Mytilus californianus
animalGroup: Invertebrates
nativeStatus: Native
diet: Phytoplankton and suspended organic particles filtered from seawater.
ecologicalRole: A filter feeder and habitat-forming species that shelters
  diverse intertidal organisms.
description: California Mussels form dense clusters on rocky shores and other
  hard coastal surfaces. Their elongated shells are dark blue-black to brown and
  often have prominent growth lines. Strong byssal threads anchor them firmly
  against waves. Mussel beds create shelter for many smaller marine organisms.
coolFacts: |-
  They attach using strong flexible threads.
  Dense beds form habitat for other animals.
  They feed by filtering particles from seawater.
threatsAndConservation: Pollution, marine heat waves, ocean acidification, and
  trampling can damage mussel beds. Avoid stepping on or removing intertidal
  wildlife.
