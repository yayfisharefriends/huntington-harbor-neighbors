slug: california-gull
commonName: California Gull
scientificName: Larus californicus
animalGroup: Birds
nativeStatus: Native
diet: Insects, fish, aquatic invertebrates, eggs, and carrion.
ecologicalRole: An adaptable omnivore and scavenger connecting inland and coastal food webs.
description: California Gulls visit Southern California coastal waters and
  wetlands, particularly outside the breeding season. Adults have gray backs,
  white heads, black wingtips, and yellowish legs. Compared with Western Gulls,
  they are typically smaller and paler-backed. They forage along beaches,
  mudflats, fields, and open water.
coolFacts: |-
  Many breed at inland lakes rather than on the coast.
  Their bills often show both red and black markings.
  They consume insects as well as aquatic prey.
threatsAndConservation: Pollution, habitat changes, and disturbance at breeding
  colonies can affect populations. Protecting wetlands and reducing shoreline
  litter help.
