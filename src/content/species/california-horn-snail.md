scientificName: Cerithideopsis californica
animalGroup: Invertebrates
nativeStatus: Native
diet: Microalgae, organic films, and detritus on marsh sediments.
ecologicalRole: A salt-marsh grazer and detritivore that also participates in
  parasite life cycles.
description: California Horn Snails are small pointed snails found on tidal
  mudflats and salt-marsh sediments. Their narrow shells have many whorls and
  blend into muddy surfaces. They slowly graze films of algae and organic
  material. They may occur in large numbers along protected marsh edges.
coolFacts: |-
  Older sources often use the name Cerithidea californica.
  They can be intermediate hosts for trematode parasites.
  They are characteristic animals of Pacific coast salt marshes.
threatsAndConservation: Marsh filling, altered tidal exchange, and polluted
  runoff threaten suitable habitat. Restoring tidal wetlands benefits these
  snails.
