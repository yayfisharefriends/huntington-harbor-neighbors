---
slug: brown-pelican
commonName: Brown Pelican
scientificName: Pelecanus occidentalis
animalGroup: Birds
nativeStatus: Native
diet: Mostly small schooling fish, including anchovies and sardines.
ecologicalRole: A nearshore fish predator and conspicuous part of coastal food webs.
description: Brown Pelicans glide close to the ocean surface and are a familiar
  sight along the Huntington Beach coast. Their long bills, expandable throat
  pouches, and broad wings make them unmistakable. They catch fish by plunging
  headfirst into the water. They often rest together on jetties, buoys, and
  other harbor structures.
coolFacts: |-
  Their populations recovered substantially after restrictions on DDT.
  They may fly in long lines just above the waves.
  Their throat pouch helps them drain water after catching fish.
threatsAndConservation: Fishing hooks and line, harmful algal blooms, and
  disturbance can injure pelicans. Dispose of fishing gear safely and give
  resting birds space.
