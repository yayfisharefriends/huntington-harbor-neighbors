slug: great-blue-heron
commonName: Great Blue Heron
scientificName: Ardea herodias
animalGroup: Birds
nativeStatus: Native
diet: Fish, amphibians, aquatic invertebrates, reptiles, and small mammals.
ecologicalRole: A large wading predator that links aquatic and shoreline food webs.
description: Great Blue Herons are imposing wading birds that hunt along
  channels, mudflats, and quiet harbor edges. Their blue-gray plumage, long
  legs, and daggerlike bills make them easy to recognize. They fly with their
  necks folded into an S shape, unlike cranes that extend their necks. They may
  stand almost motionless for long periods before catching a fish.
coolFacts: |-
  They build large stick nests, often high in trees.
  They can swallow surprisingly large fish.
  Their wingspan can exceed six feet.
threatsAndConservation: Loss of wetlands, contaminants, and disturbance at
  nesting colonies can affect these birds. Preserve wetland habitat and avoid
  approaching active nests.
