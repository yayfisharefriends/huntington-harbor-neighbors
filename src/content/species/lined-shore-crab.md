commonName: Striped Shore Crab
scientificName: Pachygrapsus crassipes
animalGroup: Invertebrates
nativeStatus: Native
diet: Algae, detritus, carrion, and small invertebrates.
ecologicalRole: An intertidal grazer and scavenger that recycles nutrients and
  provides food for shorebirds.
description: Lined Shore Crabs scuttle across rocks, crevices, and harbor edges
  in the intertidal zone. Their squarish shells are often dark green or purple
  with fine transverse lines. They move quickly sideways and hide in narrow
  cracks when disturbed. Their lined carapaces help distinguish them from other
  small shore crabs.
coolFacts: >-
  They can remain out of water for extended periods when conditions are
  suitable.

  They graze algae on rocks.

  They use crevices to avoid predators and drying out.
threatsAndConservation: Pollution, trampling, and alteration of natural
  shorelines can reduce habitat quality. Explore rocky shorelines gently and
  avoid collecting animals.
