diet: Small fish, shrimp, crabs, aquatic insects, and other small animals.
ecologicalRole: A shallow-water predator that helps regulate small fish and invertebrates.
description: Snowy Egrets stalk shallow water and mudflats with quick, delicate
  steps. Their white feathers, slim black bills, black legs, and bright yellow
  feet are distinctive. They are smaller than Great Egrets, which have yellow
  bills and black feet. They sometimes stir the water with their feet to flush
  out prey.
coolFacts: |-
  Their yellow feet are sometimes called golden slippers.
  Grow long decorative plumes during breeding season.
  Plume hunting once severely reduced egret populations.
threatsAndConservation: Wetland loss, polluted runoff, and disturbance at
  colonies can harm egrets. Wetland restoration and clean water benefit them.
