slug: california-sea-lion
commonName: California Sea Lion
scientificName: Zalophus californianus
animalGroup: Mammals
nativeStatus: Native
diet: Fish and squid, including anchovies, sardines, and other schooling species.
ecologicalRole: A major marine predator that moves nutrients between coastal
  waters and haul-out sites.
description: California Sea Lions are lively marine mammals often seen surfacing
  in coastal waters or resting on buoys and docks. They have visible external
  ear flaps, long front flippers, and whiskered snouts. Large adult males
  develop a prominent forehead crest and are much heavier than females. Unlike
  true seals, sea lions can rotate their hind flippers forward to move on land.
coolFacts: Large and in charge, you may see these guys lounging on docks near
  the fire station, or resurfacing to get more air with a distinctive "HUFF!"
  sound. Some residents affectionately call the one who resurfaces around Sunset
  Marina. Can be distinguished from seals by their external ear flaps!
threatsAndConservation: Entanglement, harmful algal blooms, disease, and
  disturbance can injure sea lions. Never feed or approach them, and report
  injured animals to trained responders.
