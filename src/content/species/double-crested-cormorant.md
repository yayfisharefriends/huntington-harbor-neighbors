slug: double-crested-cormorant
commonName: Double-crested Cormorant
scientificName: Nannopterum auritum
animalGroup: Birds
nativeStatus: Native
diet: Primarily fish.
ecologicalRole: A diving fish predator that transfers aquatic nutrients to shoreline roosts.
description: Double-crested Cormorants often swim low in harbor water or perch
  on pilings with their wings spread. They have dark plumage, long necks, hooked
  bills, and orange facial skin. They dive underwater to pursue fish and
  commonly dry their wings afterward. Small head crests may be visible during
  breeding season.
coolFacts: >-
  Can pursue fish underwater using their feet, diving up to 30 feet deep for
  their prey!

  They often gather at communal roosts, and can be seen sunning their wings to
  dry off.

  Their name refers to paired breeding-season head crests.
threatsAndConservation: Water pollution, entanglement, and disturbance at
  nesting colonies are concerns. Clean waterways and protected roosting habitat
  support these birds.
