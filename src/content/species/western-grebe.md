slug: western-grebe
commonName: Western Grebe
scientificName: Aechmophorus occidentalis
animalGroup: Birds
nativeStatus: Native
diet: Fish and some aquatic invertebrates.
ecologicalRole: A diving predator of small fish in coastal bays and inland waters.
description: Western Grebes are elegant black-and-white waterbirds with long,
  slender necks. Their dark caps extend below the eyes, and their bills are
  relatively dull yellow-green. They dive underwater to catch fish and may be
  seen in protected coastal waters during the nonbreeding season. The similar
  Clark's Grebe usually shows more white around the eye and a brighter yellow
  bill.
coolFacts: |-
  Pairs perform an elaborate rushing courtship display across the water.
  Adults may carry chicks on their backs.
  Their legs are set far back, making walking on land awkward.
threatsAndConservation: Fishing-gear entanglement, water pollution, and changes
  to breeding wetlands can harm grebes. Protecting wetland and bay habitats
  supports them.
