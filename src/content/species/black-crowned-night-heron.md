diet: Small fish, crustaceans, insects, amphibians, and other small aquatic animals.
ecologicalRole: A wetland predator that helps regulate populations of fish and

description: Black-crowned Night Herons often rest quietly in shoreline trees
  and vegetation around Southern California wetlands. Adults have a black crown
  and back, gray wings, pale underparts, and red eyes. Their stocky build and
  short neck distinguish them from taller herons. They often hunt around dusk
  and after dark.
coolFacts: |-
  Young birds are brown and streaked rather than black and gray.
  They may nest in colonies alongside other herons and egrets.
  They often wait motionless before striking at prey.
threatsAndConservation: Wetland loss, polluted runoff, and disturbance around

  vegetation, and clean water supports local populations.
