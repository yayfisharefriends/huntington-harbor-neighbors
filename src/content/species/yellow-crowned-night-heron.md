commonName: Yellow-crowned Night Heron
scientificName: Nyctanassa violacea
animalGroup: Birds
nativeStatus: Native
diet: Mostly crabs and other crustaceans, along with mollusks, insects, and
  small fish.
ecologicalRole: A coastal predator that helps regulate crab and other invertebrate populations.
description: Yellow-crowned Night Herons are less frequently encountered in
  Southern California than Black-crowned Night Herons. Adults have a dark face
  with bold white markings, a pale crown, and gray body plumage. Their stout
  bills are well suited to catching crabs along shallow shorelines. They may
  forage in low light near marshes and protected coastal waters.
coolFacts: |-
  Crabs are a major part of their diet.
  Juveniles have brown streaked plumage.
  They often nest in trees or shrubs near water.
threatsAndConservation: Coastal wetland loss, pollution, and disturbance reduce
  suitable habitat. Conserving estuaries and undisturbed roosting areas benefits
  these birds.
