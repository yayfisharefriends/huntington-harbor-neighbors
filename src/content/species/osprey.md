---
slug: osprey
commonName: Osprey
scientificName: Pandion haliaetus
animalGroup: Birds
nativeStatus: Native
diet: Almost entirely fish.
ecologicalRole: A specialized fish predator that links aquatic and terrestrial food webs.
description: Ospreys patrol coastal waterways searching for fish near the
  surface. They have brown upperparts, pale underparts, and a bold dark stripe
  through each eye. Their long wings bend noticeably at the wrist in flight.
  They plunge feet-first into the water and may carry captured fish to a perch.
coolFacts: |-
  Reversible outer toes help them grip fish.
  Their feet have rough pads for holding slippery prey.
  Pairs may reuse large stick nests, and can be seen perching above PCH!
threatsAndConservation: Disturbance at nests, entanglement, and contaminants can
  affect Ospreys. Protect nesting sites and maintain healthy fish habitats.
